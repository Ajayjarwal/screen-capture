Tests used isolated Chromium-based profiles. Test copies had temporary
host access to emulate a real toolbar click. The delivered extension has
no host_permissions and does not request new permissions for this feature.
Editable/private Google Sheets, arbitrary cross-origin frames, and automatic
capture of every worksheet tab are outside this mode. It captures the active
published worksheet; choose another worksheet tab and capture it separately.
